Why Good Design Starts With Questions

Written by Design Thinking Explained

Published January 2026

Good design begins by asking the right questions. Designers need to understand the problem, the users, and the goal before they start creating solutions.

By exploring different perspectives and challenging assumptions, better and more meaningful results can be achieved.
